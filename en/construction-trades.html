Day-to-day

What businesses tell us again and again

Not a criticism, just the usual state of play: office, job site and customer calls run in parallel, and the tools do not keep up.

Inquiries and quotes scattered

Emails, WhatsApp, notes, Excel. Who asked for what and which version is current often lives in one person's head.

A website that does not convert

Nice photos, but no clear path to inquire. Prospects bounce or call when nobody can pick up.

Schedules and sites in someone's head

Who is where, what is still missing, when the subcontractor arrives: the overview sits with you, not in a system.

Job costing too late

Materials, hours, extras: you reconcile at month-end instead of knowing whether the job still pays as you go.

Follow-up slips

Open quotes, reminders, call-backs: day-to-day work buries them, and the job goes to someone else.

What we build for you

Concrete relief, not a tool zoo

We look at how you work today and only ship what actually helps. Often a solid website plus a clear inquiry and quote path is enough.

A website that brings inquiries

Clear services, strong references, forms and calls to action that fit. Prospects find their way to you fast, including on mobile from the site.

Inquiry and quote flow

From first inquiry to finished quote: structured, traceable, without copy-paste across five programs.

Simple automation

Reminders for open quotes, routing new inquiries to the right person, status updates. Low effort, noticeable effect.

Optional app or portal

If you want it: customer portal, job-site overview or an internal app for the team. Only when it truly simplifies your day.
