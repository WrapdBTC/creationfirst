What we build for you

Six areas, one contact. Every project starts with a free call and ends with something your team uses every day.

Websites

Websites that load fast, work well on a phone and lead visitors to get in touch. With a clear structure, plain-language copy and a form that asks the right questions.

Usually a delivery sprint. A lean website is often done in 1–3 weeks.

Ask about a website
  • Custom design instead of a template look
  • Built for phones and tablets
  • Fast load times, good Core Web Vitals
  • SEO basics from day one
  • Content you can edit yourself later
  • A contact form that lands straight with you

Apps & software

Web apps, customer portals and internal tools for workflows that no off-the-shelf program handles well. We connect them to what you already use instead of rebuilding everything.

Usually 1–3 sprints, so you have something usable early.

Ask about an app project
  • Web apps and progressive web apps
  • APIs and integrations with existing systems
  • Maintainable architecture that can grow
  • Automated tests and code reviews
  • Documentation for your team
  • Handover with deployment and a walkthrough

AI & automation

Chatbots, assistants and automations that answer inquiries, write drafts and pass data between your tools. Built into your workflows, so your team has nothing new to learn.

Often starts with an AI audit, then gets built in sprints.

Ask about an AI audit See examples
  • Chatbots for your website and WhatsApp
  • Email and document automation
  • Phone assistants and voice agents
  • Custom AI integrations
  • Connected to the tools you already use
  • Data protection clarified and documented upfront

Online shops

Shops that work for buyers and for you: clear product pages, a fast checkout and clean integration with payment, shipping and inventory.

Usually 1–2 sprints, depending on your range and integrations.

Ask about a shop
  • Custom shop design
  • Payment and shipping integration
  • Product and inventory management
  • Fast checkout, especially on mobile
  • Multilingual shops possible
  • Reports on orders and drop-offs

Branding & SEO

A brand people recognize and content that Google finds. From logo and colors to copy for the things your customers are actually searching for.

Often part of a website project, also bookable on its own.

Ask about branding or SEO
  • Logo and visual identity
  • Design system and style guide
  • Technical SEO audit
  • Keyword and content strategy
  • Local SEO for your region
  • Copy for your website and social media

Maintenance & hosting

Launch is where the real work starts. We keep your website and apps up to date, secure and fast, and you have a contact when something gets stuck.

As ongoing care or a small maintenance package, as needed.

Ask about ongoing care
  • Updates and security patches
  • Monitoring and backups
  • Hosting advice and setup
  • Fast help when something breaks
  • Flexible support packages
  • Small changes without a new project
