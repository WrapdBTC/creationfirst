About CreationFirst

Digital solutions built with craft and clarity

We're a digital agency for web design, software development, digital branding, and AI-driven growth — with one clear standard: quality over quantity.

Our Approach

Why CreationFirst exists

Many digital projects don't fail because of the technology — they fail because of unclear communication, bloated processes, and agencies juggling too many clients at once. That's exactly what we do differently.

Led by Pierre Schulteis, CreationFirst stands for short feedback loops, honest advice, and cleanly executed digital products — whether that's a website, an online store, custom software, or getting started with AI in a way that actually helps.

We've seen even established companies fall behind simply because they don't have the time to explore what AI-driven automation can do for them. That's exactly where we step in — without you having to spend your own time or resources.

Because we consult in German, English and Croatian, we support clients across the German-speaking region as well as Southeast Europe — with the same standard of quality.
