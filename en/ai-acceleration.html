What we build

Six building blocks that pay off in almost any business

On their own or combined. Each comes with a typical example, so you can picture how it might look for you.

Chatbots & customer service

Answers common questions on your website or in WhatsApp instantly, including evenings and weekends.

Example: A trade business lets questions about prices and free slots be answered automatically and only gets the real inquiries.

Emails & documents

Quotes, invoices and standard replies are prepared automatically. You just check and send.

Example: An inquiry email turns into a quote draft with the right line items within minutes.

Phone assistant

Takes calls, answers standard questions and forwards urgent ones, even when nobody can pick up.

Example: A practice no longer misses appointment requests, because the assistant takes them and passes them on.

Workflows between tools

Data moves automatically between inbox, spreadsheet, calendar and CRM. No more copy and paste.

Example: A new order lands automatically in the calendar, in accounting and with the right employee.

Reports & analytics

Numbers from different sources are pulled together and turned into one clear report.

Example: Several stores no longer reconcile stock in Excel, the report arrives every Monday on its own.

Custom integrations

If no standard solution fits, we build a connection to your existing system.

Example: An assistant that knows your internal documents and answers questions from the team.
