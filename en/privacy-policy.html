Privacy Policy

We're glad you're interested in this website. Protecting your personal data matters to us. Below we explain how personal data is processed when you use this website, in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data Controller

The data controller within the meaning of the GDPR is:
Creationfirst
Sarajevska Ulica 24, 21000 Split, Croatia
Email: info@creationfirst.io

2. Hosting of this Website

This website is hosted via GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. (or GitHub B.V. for users in the EU). When you visit this website, the hosting provider automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, date and time of access, browser used, and the page requested (server log files). This processing is based on our legitimate interest in providing a secure and functional website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More information is available in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

3. Cookies & Tracking

This website does not use analytics or tracking cookies and does not embed advertising or social media services that set cookies. No user profiles are created.

4. Contact Form

If you use the contact form on this website, the data you enter (e.g., name, email address, phone number, message) is processed to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Submission is handled via a Cloudflare Worker we operate (Cloudflare, Inc.). The request is then forwarded to our internal notification channel (Discord) so we can reply promptly. More information is available in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Alternatively, you can contact us directly by email, in which case we process your details to handle your inquiry.

5. Live Chat

On selected pages we offer an optional live chat. If you use it, your messages and any contact details you provide (e.g. email or phone) are processed via the same Cloudflare Worker to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). Replies may use AI assistance (Cloudflare Workers AI); if contact details or buying intent are detected, an internal notification may be sent.

6. External Fonts & Resources

This website uses only system-native fonts and does not embed external fonts (e.g. Google Fonts). Technical requests for the contact form and live chat run through Cloudflare Workers as described in sections 4 and 5.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to free information about your stored personal data, its origin and recipients, and the purpose of its processing, as well as a right to rectification, restriction, or deletion of this data. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

8. Contact for Privacy Questions

If you have questions about data protection, reach us at info@creationfirst.io.
