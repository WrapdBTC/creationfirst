Privacy policy

Protecting your personal data matters to us. Here you can find out which data is processed when you use this website and which rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data controller

The data controller within the meaning of the GDPR is:
CreationFirst, Pierre Schulteis
Sarajevska ulica 24, 21000 Split, Croatia
Email: info@creationfirst.io

2. Hosting of this website

This website is hosted via GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. (or GitHub B.V. for users in the EU). When you visit this website, the hosting provider automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, date and time of access, the browser used and the page requested (server log files). This is based on our legitimate interest in providing a secure and functional website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More information is available in GitHub’s privacy statement.

3. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics or tracking cookies and does not embed advertising or social media services that set cookies. No user profiles are created.

4. Storage in your browser

Some features remember settings directly in your browser (local storage or session storage). This data does not leave your device and is not sent to us:

  • your choice between light and dark design,
  • best score, leaderboard and sound setting in the Snake game,
  • a random conversation ID while you use the live chat in the same tab.

You can delete this data at any time in your browser settings.

5. Contact form

If you use the contact form, we process the data you enter (e.g. name, email address, phone number, message) to handle your inquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Submission is handled via a Cloudflare Worker we operate (Cloudflare, Inc.). The inquiry is then forwarded to our internal notification channel (Discord) so we can get back to you promptly. More information is available in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. You can also email us directly, in which case we process your details to handle your inquiry.

6. Live chat

This website offers an optional live chat. If you use it, your messages and any contact details you provide (e.g. email or phone) are processed via the same Cloudflare Worker to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). Replies may use AI assistance (Cloudflare Workers AI). If contact details or buying intent are detected, an internal notification may be sent.

7. Fonts and external resources

This website uses only system fonts and does not embed external fonts (e.g. Google Fonts). Technical requests from the contact form and live chat run through Cloudflare Workers as described in sections 5 and 6.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right at any time to free information about your stored personal data, its origin and recipients, and the purpose of processing. You also have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority.

9. Contact for privacy questions

If you have questions about data protection, reach us at info@creationfirst.io.
