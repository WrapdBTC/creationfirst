About us

A small team for web, apps and automation

CreationFirst is a digital agency based in Split. We build websites, apps and automations for small and mid-sized businesses in the DACH region and Croatia, in German, English and Croatian.

Why we exist

So digital projects do not fail over small things

Many projects do not fail because of technology. They fail because of unclear communication, bloated processes and agencies juggling too many clients at once.

At the same time, we see good businesses fall behind because they simply lack the time to look into AI and automation. We want to solve both: short paths and technology that takes work off your plate.
