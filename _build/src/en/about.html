About CreationFirst

Short paths, honest advice, clean work

CreationFirst is a digital studio based in Split. We build websites, apps and automations for small and mid-sized businesses in the DACH region and Croatia, in German, English and Croatian.

Why CreationFirst exists

Founded so digital projects do not fail over small things

Many projects do not fail because of technology. They fail because of unclear communication, bloated processes and agencies juggling too many clients at once.

At the same time, we see good businesses fall behind because they simply lack the time to look into AI and automation. CreationFirst is meant to solve both: short paths and technology that takes work off your plate.

Pierre Schulteis founded CreationFirst and runs it from Split. He is your contact from the first call to handover and is personally responsible for every project.
